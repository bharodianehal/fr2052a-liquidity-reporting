# FR 2052a Liquidity Risk Reporting Platform

End-to-end **Databricks** demo implementing the Federal Reserve **FR 2052a Complex Institution Liquidity Monitoring Report** (Version 6G) with **LCR** (Liquidity Coverage Ratio) calculation per 12 CFR 249 Appendix VI.

**Industry:** financial services - banking (US bank holding companies, Category I-IV). **Outcome:** regulatory reporting and
liquidity risk - file the daily FR 2052a on time with every number traceable to source, and monitor the LCR.

---

> **Built on Databricks.** This project runs entirely on the Databricks Data Intelligence Platform: Lakeflow (Declarative
> Pipelines with Auto Loader, Jobs), Unity Catalog (schemas, volumes, tags, masks, grants, lineage, metric views, functions,
> model registry), Lakebase (Postgres with synced tables), MLflow and Model Serving, AI Functions and Vector Search, a Genie
> space, AI/BI dashboards and Databricks Apps, deployed with Databricks Asset Bundles. To run it you need **your own
> Databricks workspace** with serverless compute, Lakebase and Foundation Model API access. You configure your own catalog,
> SQL warehouse and workspace values in the gitignored settings files (see [Deploy to a new workspace](#deploy-to-a-new-workspace-databricks-asset-bundles)); no workspace values,
> tokens or ids are in this repo.

## The business problem

US banks with $100B+ in assets (Category I-IV) must file the Federal Reserve's **FR 2052a Complex Institution
Liquidity Monitoring Report**, daily for the largest firms. They must also show they meet the **Liquidity Coverage Ratio**
(HQLA / 30-day stressed net cash outflows >= 100%, 12 CFR 249). In practice the data comes from five or more
source systems (core banking, loan origination, custody, trading, treasury). Every PID, counterparty, collateral
class and maturity bucket must be classified to 6G rules, then validated, reconciled and turned into XML, and
treasury needs the same numbers for the LCR. Teams spend their mornings on spreadsheets and email chasing data-quality
breaks, and supervisors expect lineage from each reported number back to source.

This build runs that journey end to end on Databricks for a synthetic US bank holding company (Acme Financial Holdings,
Category I) with **three reporting entities in one 6G file** - `ENT001` consolidated BHC, `ENT002` the bank (solo), `ENT003`
the broker-dealer (solo) - over **39 business days (2026-01-02 to 2026-02-27)**, from source-system extracts to the three 6G
XML files the Fed's FRS accepts, with the Fed's post-submission checks run before filing, numbered reporting runs, business
review, governed adjustments and sign-off. **AI is Databricks-native only**: AI Functions, a Genie Agent, Genie One skills,
managed MCP servers - it proposes, a person decides.

## End-to-end journey: source data to the Fed 6G submission

| Stage | What happens | Where |
|-------|--------------|-------|
| **0. App decisions** | Decisions people made in the app (AI proposals accepted / rejected, customer classifications, product code mappings) are brought from Lakebase into Delta before the day is processed. | `notebooks/00` |
| **1. Source extracts** | Source systems (core deposits, loans, custody, trading, treasury, collateral, GL, reg-calc) land daily CSVs in the Volume `raw.landing`. They carry the bank's own **source product code**, subtype, pay/receive direction, **customer id** and **collateral id** - not FR 2052a codes. Lakeflow (Auto Loader) loads them into `raw.*`. | `notebooks/01`, `pipelines/bronze_ingest.py` |
| **2. Data quality gate** | **Feed completeness** per source feed (data contract: records vs the trailing 5-day median, `dq.feed_monitor`), then SQL rules from `reference.rules_dq` (completeness, range, referential checks against the product catalogue, customer and security masters, duplicates). ERROR rules quarantine; clean records go to `conformed.source_position`. | `notebooks/02` |
| **3. PID derivation & enrichment** | Attribute-level transformation: Counterparty, G-SIB, Internal from the **customer master**; Collateral Class and HQLA level from the **security master**; the **PID** from `reference.rules_pid_derivation` (ordered SQL conditions per source product code, first match wins, rule id kept for lineage); Appendix IV-a maturity buckets; risk weight to the 6G decimal. Unmatched records go to an exception queue. Synthetic data is checked against an answer key. **Calculation layer** (aligned to the Oracle LRS FR 2052a intermediate calculations): standard product types per source system, option-adjusted maturity (earliest for outflows, latest for inflows), effective drawdown date, operational / excess deposit split, central-bank reserve split, FDIC insured / uninsured allocation, placed and received collateral from collateral links (lowest-liquidity piece wins), substitutable collateral, lendable value by pledge venue, weekend flows. **Entity scoping**: each Silver position is reported by every reporting entity whose legal-entity tree contains its booking entity; counterparties inside the scope are eliminated (`audit.intercompany_elimination`), affiliates outside it are Internal with the Internal Counterparty. | `notebooks/03` |
| **4. LCR** | 12 CFR 249 with the 74 Appendix VI rules, Level 2 caps and the 75% inflow cap, **per LCR entity** (ENT001, ENT002); each ratio is reported as that entity's **S.L.6**. | `notebooks/04` |
| **5. Report build** | Silver aggregated into the **13 FR 2052a data tables + Comments** on the 6G report key (every non-amount field); non-6G currencies converted to USD with Converted = true; records rounding below 0.01 dropped; every filed record keeps its positions in `reg_2052a.record_lineage` (drill-down); **approved adjustments** (maker / checker, from the app) applied on top; **FR Y-15 Schedule G (STWF)** from the FR 2052a data elements (Appendix VII) with MDRM codes; GL reconciliation per legal entity and FR 2052a-vs-LCR per LCR entity. | `notebooks/05` |
| **6. Validation & 6G files** | Appendix rules (II-a to V, Field Definitions) on the tables; **Primary**, **Reduced** (month-end) and **Comment** XML generated from the XSD layout; **XSD validation** against the Fed's 6G schemas; all **652 Business Requirements rules** plus the BR General file rules (duplicate report key, file content, naming). the **Fed's 14 FR 2052a post-submission validation checks** (PSV-01..14: weekend maturities, internal on consolidated, intercompany symmetry, lendable > market, large haircuts, missing products by entity type, duplicates, ...). ERROR failures block release. Every generation is a numbered **reporting run**; the 6G header version is one more than the versions already submitted; the **report template version** comes from `ref_report_template` by activation date. | `notebooks/06` |
| **7. AI assist** | Databricks AI Functions propose, people decide in the app: `ai_classify` + `ai_similarity` + `ai_query` classify new customers (Counterparty, G-SIB parent); `ai_query` maps unknown bank product codes, explains PID exceptions and failed checks, drafts variance commentary and S.C comments; `ai_forecast` gives the expected range per entity x table / PID (anomalies). Scored against the synthetic answer keys in MLflow. | `notebooks/09`, `10`, `12` |
| **8. Business review & sign-off** | The app's **reporting workbench**: calendar and feeds, AI review queue, per-entity data sets, Fed checks, variance, drill-down to source records, adjustments (preparer != approver), PID dictionary, Genie. Run control: approve, request override (BLOCKED run, documented reason, approved by someone else), reject, request a new run, mark submitted (not the approver). | `app/` |
| Serving, governance, NL | Lakebase sync for the app; UC tags, mask and grants; Genie space (Genie Agent); Genie One skills; UC functions served by the managed MCP server. | `notebooks/07`, `08`, `11`, `deploy/`, `genie/skills/`, |

**PIDs, not MDRM.** The FR 2052a has no MDRM codes: the 6G schema and the Instructions identify every value by its product ID
(PID) and field values. (The Fed's MDRM dictionary still lists the pre-2015 FR 2052a line-item template, mnemonic LMMA; it
is not what firms file.) MDRM codes are used only for the FR Y-15 Schedule G items computed from FR 2052a data.

**Sources of truth.** `reference_data/` is built by `scripts/spec/build_reference_data.py` from the FR 2052a Instructions PDF
(business meaning) and the Fed's **6G Schema and Sample Files package** (XSD + Business Requirements workbook, 2021-12-28).
Every value that goes into the XML - product names, enumeration spellings, maturity bucket durations, Appendix II-a
combinations, the G-SIB list, element order - comes from the 6G package; the build fails if the two sources disagree.
The firm's own mapping (product catalogue, PID rules, customer and security masters, firm include XSD values) is built by
`scripts/firm/build_firm_reference.py`. The 6G package itself is not in git; its two XSDs are in `schema_6g/`, which the bundle deploys for notebook 06.

**Execution evidence** (text: executed notebooks with outputs, job runs, query results, model output, API responses, test output) is in [`evidence/`](evidence/README.md), one file per stage.

## Architecture

```
Source CSVs ─> Lakeflow ─> DQ gate ─> PID derivation ─> Silver ─┬─> LCR engine ─┐
(raw.landing)  raw.*       dq.*       + enrichment      conformed └──────────────┴─> 13 FR 2052a tables ─> validation ─> 6G XML
                                       (rules_pid_derivation,                        + Comments            (XSD + 652 BR    Primary / Reduced / Comment

                 \__________ Unity Catalog: tags, comments, mask, grants, lineage ___________/        Lakebase ─> Databricks App, Genie
```

## Deploy to a new workspace (Databricks Asset Bundles)

Everything is deployed by two Databricks Asset Bundles; there are no hand-made objects. Prerequisites: Databricks CLI (with bundles),
a profile for the workspace, a catalog and a SQL warehouse (Node.js 18+ only to rebuild the frontend).

| Bundle | Resources |
|---|---|
| Platform - `databricks.yml` + `resources/` | UC schemas (10) and volumes (4) (`catalog.yml`); Lakeflow pipeline (`pipeline.yml`); the 6 jobs (`jobs.yml`); Lakebase project, endpoint and database (`lakebase.yml`); secret scope and Vector Search endpoint (`ai.yml`); the 2 AI/BI dashboards (`dashboards.yml`) |
| App - `app/databricks.yml` + `app/resources/` | the Databricks App; the Genie space (`genie_space.yml`, generated by `deploy/genie_space.py`); the Vector Search index, the agents' serving endpoint and its Lakebase role (`agents.yml`) |

Two kinds of asset are not bundle resource types; bundle jobs create them: the agents' service principal and its OAuth secret
(job `fr2052a-register-agents`, task `agents_identity`), and the Lakebase synced tables with their source views (job
`fr2052a-pipeline`, task `lakebase_tables` - they need the data). The pipeline job also loads the regulatory text the agents cite
(`regulatory_text`, from `reference_data/regtext/`) and uploads the Genie One skills (`genie_skills`).

The app bundle is deployed after the first build: the Genie space, the index and the serving endpoint need the tables and the
registered model the build creates. Workspace values are bundle variables, kept out of git in
`.databricks/bundle/<target>/variable-overrides.json` and `app/.databricks/bundle/<target>/variable-overrides.json`
(templates: `variable-overrides.example.json` in the root and in `app/`).

```bash
# 0. settings: copy the templates and fill them in (the Fed XSDs are in schema_6g/)
cp variable-overrides.example.json .databricks/bundle/dev/variable-overrides.json
cp app/variable-overrides.example.json app/.databricks/bundle/dev/variable-overrides.json; cp .env.example .env

# 1. platform bundle, then the first build (more Lakebase CU for the first full load of the synced tables)
databricks bundle deploy -t dev -p <profile> --var lakebase_max_cu=4
databricks bundle run fr2052a_pipeline -t dev -p <profile>
databricks bundle deploy -t dev -p <profile>                       # back to 1 CU

# 2. the agents: service principal + OAuth secret, register and promote a version (prints agents_sp_client_id and the version)
databricks bundle run fr2052a_register_agents -t dev -p <profile> --params promote=true

# 3. app bundle: put agents_sp_client_id, agents_model_version, lakebase_host, workspace_host and the two dashboard ids
#    (databricks bundle summary) into app/.databricks/bundle/dev/variable-overrides.json
cd app      # the built frontend (static/) is in git; after a frontend change: cd frontend && npm install && npm run build && cd ..
databricks bundle deploy -t dev -p <profile> && databricks bundle run fr2052a -t dev -p <profile> && cd ..

# 4. grants for the app's and the agents' service principals: set app_sp_client_id and agents_sp_client_id in the platform
#    overrides, deploy again; notebooks 07 / 08 grant them on the next run of a day (or of the pipeline)
databricks bundle deploy -t dev -p <profile>
```
After a promotion of the agents (job `fr2052a-register-agents`, `promote=true`) set `agents_model_version` in the app overrides to
the new version, so the next app deploy keeps the endpoint on it. After changing the Genie space definition in `deploy/genie_space.py`,
run `python deploy/genie_space.py` (writes `app/resources/genie_space.yml`) and deploy the app bundle.
Reference data is rebuilt only when the spec or the firm mapping changes: `scripts/spec/build_reference_data.py`,
`scripts/firm/build_firm_reference.py`, `scripts/spec/check_reference_data.py`.

The app has nine resources: `sql-warehouse`, `fr2052a-lakebase` (postgres, CAN_CONNECT_AND_CREATE), `genie-space` (CAN_RUN),
`daily-job`, `revalidate-job`, `generate-job`, `demo-reset-job` (CAN_MANAGE_RUN), `ai-model` (`databricks-claude-sonnet-4-6`) and `agents-model`
(`fr2052a-agents`, CAN_QUERY). Notebook 08 grants its service principal read on the Silver/Gold schemas so Genie can query them;
notebook 07 grants it read on the Lakebase tables and write on the app-owned tables, and revokes UPDATE / DELETE on the audit trail
(`agent_events`, `break_decisions`, `ai_decisions`). The agents' service principal `fr2052a-agents` gets the same read (plus the
Lakebase source views and write on the MLflow trace tables) from notebooks 08 and 07, its Lakebase role from the app bundle.

Tests: `python -m pytest tests -q` (62 offline unit tests) and `python scripts/qa/e2e_qa.py` (the live QA suite, `scripts/qa/`).

**Demo environment.** Every demo date (Feb 23-27) has work at each step - a short feed (Collect), quarantined records (Data quality),
a record with no PID rule and new customers / product codes (PID enrichment), check exceptions and comments (Validate). Two demo-only
controls, not part of the product: the send icon in the header (the source re-sends a short feed for the dates you pick) and a faint
**reset** link at the bottom of the sidebar (job `fr2052a-demo-reset`, notebook 17: removes everything decided on the demo dates and
re-runs them).

## Databricks-native AI

Everything AI runs on Databricks; nothing calls an outside model or service.

| Capability | Used for | Where |

### Regulatory text for the agents
`reference_data/regtext/reg_text_chunks.jsonl` (built by the chunkers in `scripts/regtext/`): FR 2052a Instructions (147 product definitions + page passages) and 12 CFR 249 (eCFR, by section) -> volume `reference.docs`, table `reference.reg_text_chunks` (task `regulatory_text` of `fr2052a-pipeline`, `deploy/regulatory_text_index.py`) -> Vector Search index `reference.reg_text_index` on endpoint `fr2052a-vs` (databricks-gte-large-en; both in the bundles). The agents look up the product definitions and the CFR paragraph exactly and use `vector_search()` for the closest passages.


### Business calendar and settlement dates
`reference.ref_bank_holidays` (Federal Reserve holidays 2025-2035, `scripts/firm/build_bank_holidays.py`). Notebook 03 rolls every maturity, forward-start and effective-maturity date that falls on a weekend or holiday to the next business day before bucketing (Instructions p.21: buckets are calendar days after T, on the date the flow actually settles); the contractual date stays in `contract_maturity_date`. Notebook 06 (PSV-01, month-end), notebook 14 (corrected dates) and the app (T+2 due date, the rule agent's business-day test) use the same calendar.


## Serving, AI and MLflow (v10)

- **The app reads Lakebase only**: 62 synced tables (`app_serving`, Delta -> Postgres, one pipeline; incremental for the large position tables) and its own tables in `public` (decisions, sign-off). The SQL warehouse is for the pipeline, the AI/BI dashboards and Genie.
- **The AI agents are a registered MLflow model**: `<catalog>.ai_assist.fr2052a_agents` (pyfunc). Notebook 15 (last task of `fr2052a-daily` / `fr2052a-revalidate`) calls its `@champion` version for every open item and stores the proposals; notebook 16 registers and promotes versions; notebook 10 evaluates them (checker feedback, `mlflow.genai.evaluate`). Every proposal is an MLflow trace (Unity Catalog trace tables in `ai_assist`).
