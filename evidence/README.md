# Execution evidence (text)

Everything here is real output of the build in a Databricks workspace - executed notebooks with their cell outputs, job run results,
query results, model output, API responses and test output - captured by `deploy/capture_evidence.py` (and the QA scripts) on
2026-11-04 and 2026-11-05. All data is synthetic (generated by notebook 01); no customer data. Workspace-specific identifiers are
replaced by placeholders when the evidence is captured: `<workspace>`, `<workspace-id>`, `<catalog>`, `<app>`, `<lakebase-host>`,
`<experiment-id>`, the two service principals, `<uuid-N>` / `<object-id-N>` for other object ids, and `userN@example.com` for people.
Job and run ids are kept (counters inside the workspace).

**The problem (banking - liquidity regulatory reporting):** a US bank holding company must file the Federal Reserve's **FR 2052a** Complex
Institution Liquidity Monitoring Report (the 6G XML) by T+2 business days, and compute its **Liquidity Coverage Ratio**. The data comes from
many source systems, every position must be classified to the Fed's product IDs (PIDs) and attributes, checked against 650+ Fed business
rules, reconciled to the GL and the LCR, reviewed and signed off with four eyes. Here the AI agents do the work (the maker) and a person
checks and finalises it (the checker).
